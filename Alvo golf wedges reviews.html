Groove Physics:

The science of spin generation relies heavily on the physical interaction between the golf ball's soft urethane cover and the sharp edges of a club's milled groove walls. During impact, the massive force of the swing compresses the ball against the metal face, forcing the cover material into the open channels of the precision-milled grooves. This mechanical interlocking creates the friction required to launch the ball with high spin rates and a controlled, lower trajectory. Our detailed Alvo golf wedges reviews database shows that the full-face groove configuration maintains this critical friction even on extreme toe or heel strikes, which often happen when sweeping through thick rough. Without these deep, sharp channels, moisture and grass would lubricate the contact point, causing the ball to slide up the face with low spin, resulting in an unpredictable flyer that sails past the target. Clean grooves act like high-performance tires on a wet road, gripping the ball to deliver total control over distance and stopping power.
